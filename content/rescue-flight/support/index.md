For help with Rescue Flight, email Jesse Bounds at [bounds@mail.grokbot.com](mailto:bounds@mail.grokbot.com).

Include your device model, operating-system version, game version and what happened. If you can, give the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help, but remove personal information first. Never send your Apple Account password.

## Controls

**iPhone and iPad:** Drag the on-screen joystick to fly. Release it to hover. Land on a rescue pad to collect people, then take them home. Use FIRE to shoot, or enable Auto gunner in Settings. Settings also lets you move the joystick to the other side. A compatible game controller or keyboard can be used.

**Apple Vision Pro:** Pair a controller with an extended gamepad profile, such as a compatible Xbox or PlayStation controller. A controller is required to fly. Menus and settings work without one. Play seated and use the button labels shown for your controller. Enter the hangar, then select START FLIGHT. The left stick pitches and banks; the right stick turns and climbs. Looking around does not steer the helicopter.

If movement feels uncomfortable, pause or leave the game. Vision Pro Settings includes comfort flight, a vignette, cockpit-only rescue scenes and seat adjustments. Use Recenter in the pause menu to reset your seated position.

## Saved progress and iCloud

When iCloud is available, the game uses your private iCloud database to share progress, tokens, hangar items and supported settings between your devices. There is no separate game sign-in.

If progress does not appear on another device:

1. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account and that iCloud is available for the game.
2. Check the internet connection and available iCloud storage.
3. Open the game on each device and check its sync status. Use Retry if a sync error offers that action.
4. If the problem continues, contact support with the status message and which devices are affected.

You can play offline. Pending progress waits for a connection. Spending shared tokens and resetting shared progress require a connection. A local profile used without iCloud stays separate from your iCloud profile.

Do not reset progress, remove cloud data or delete the app as a first troubleshooting step. These actions can remove data. Reset progress starts a new progress period; it does not erase all retained sync records. See the [privacy policy](/rescue-flight/privacy/) for data controls.

## Sound and accessibility

Check the device's volume and the game's master, effects, music and radio controls. Captions and reduced flashes are available in Settings. On Apple Vision Pro, controller vibration and comfort options can also be adjusted there.

## Usage sharing and privacy

Usage sharing is optional. In Settings → Privacy, use **Share usage data** to enable or disable it on the current device. Turning it off does not disable iCloud saves. Read the [privacy policy](/rescue-flight/privacy/) for what is sent and how to request help with your data.
